Red teaming is about thinking like an attacker — not to cause harm, but to understand exactly how a real threat actor would move through an environment. This section documents offensive security labs covering Command & Control infrastructure, initial access techniques, lateral movement, post-exploitation, and adversary simulation.

All work is conducted in controlled lab environments (Hack The Box, TryHackMe, home lab). Findings are mapped to MITRE ATT&CK tactics and techniques where applicable.
